Send to Ledger: a Chrome and Firefox extension that sends files from any website straight into this inbox (or to Auto sort), without saving them to your computer. Right-click a download link and choose Send to Ledger…, or drop a file on its toolbar button.
  1. Chrome, Edge, Brave: unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  2. Firefox: open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox → Load Temporary Add-on… → pick manifest.json in the unzipped folder (until Firefox restarts; to keep it, sign it for yourself on addons.mozilla.org, see the README).
  3. In the extension's Options, enter , press Save, allow access, and press Test.
